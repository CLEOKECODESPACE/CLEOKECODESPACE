### 🚀 MetaPlusAI — AI Business Communication Platform

<div align="center">
  <img src="https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/74038190/225813708-98b745f2-7d22-48cf-9150-083f1b00d6c9.gif" width="420" />
</div>

**MetaPlusAI** is Africa’s most advanced AI-powered business communication platform.  
It unifies **WhatsApp Business API**, **Facebook Messenger**, **Instagram DMs**, intelligent automation, and real-time analytics into one powerful dashboard.

**Key Capabilities:**
- 🤖 AI chatbots with GPT integration
- 💬 Unified multi-platform messaging
- 📊 Real-time analytics & insights
- 💳 Payments (Stripe · PayPal · M-Pesa · Binance Pay)
- 🎯 Campaign & lead management
